About the index & metadata

This index covers the unique links in Paul Graham’s essay list, including two text chapters. It does not claim to cover every page on his website.

Dates come from essay headers. “September 2026” means the month is known, not the day. Missing dates stay unavailable. Word counts exclude site navigation and include the recognized body and footnotes. Reading time assumes 225 words per minute.

Metadata updates daily through GitHub Actions. The RSS feed checks the original index when its hourly cache expires. A source outage keeps a previous feed snapshot available. View feed status · JSON Feed

The updater fetches pages to calculate metadata and discards their text. Articles are not mirrored here. All essay links open the author’s original pages.
